06 The dates that matter

Tax payments must be made on or before the due date.

Due on a weekend or public holiday? Pay on the next working day. GST is the exception: it is due on the last working day of the period. Your own dates may differ; we will confirm them.

Good to know first

Short answers. For anything specific to you, book a chat.

What happens when I first get in touch?

We will ask a few questions about your situation, such as your income, any property, a business or a trust, and arrange a time to talk it through. The first consultation is free on all matters.

Can I come to the office?

Yes, at 620 Manukau Road in Epsom, Monday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm or Saturday 11:30am to 3:00pm, with abundant free on-site parking.

I owe Inland Revenue money. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and IRD audits, negotiate arrears for GST, PAYE and income tax, and respond to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

Can you look after a family trust?

Yes. Family trust management and advisory is one of our services, and our staff are trained in trust and estate management.

What is tax pooling?

Tax pooling through Tax Management NZ (TMNZ) lets you pay provisional tax when it suits you.
