For New Zealand-based borrowers on salary or wages: 12% of every dollar over the repayment threshold.

Pay period

KiwiSaver contributions

What you and your employer put in over a year. The default and minimum employer rate is 3.5% from 1 April 2026, rising to 4% from 1 April 2028.

ESCT bands: 10.5% to $18,720, 17.5% to $64,200, 30% to $93,720, 33% to $216,000, 39% over. KiwiSaver changes on Inland Revenue
