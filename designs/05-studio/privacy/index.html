Privacy

How this website handles the information you give it. Tax Professionals Ltd follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

This website belongs to Tax Professionals Ltd, 620 Manukau Road, Epsom, Auckland 1023. When this statement says "we" or "us", it means Tax Professionals Ltd.

What the enquiry form collects

The enquiry form on our contact page and homepage asks for:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to give it
  • the topic your enquiry is about, if you choose one
  • your message

When you press Send, your note is either delivered to us through a form service, or your own email app opens with the note ready for you to send to info@taxprofessionals.co.nz. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

How we use your enquiry

We use the details you send only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to provide the services you ask for.

Please do not send IRD numbers, bank details or other sensitive documents through the form. We can talk about how best to share them once we have spoken.

Calculators, checklist and chat

  • Calculators. The tax, take-home pay, GST and provisional tax calculators work entirely in your browser. The numbers you type never leave your device.
  • Checklist. The ticks on the "what to bring" checklist are saved only on your own device, in your browser's storage, so they are still there when you come back. Clear them at any time with the Clear ticks button.
  • Pay an invoice. The invoice number helper keeps the number on your screen only. Card payments are made on Ezidebit's secure payment page, which has its own privacy terms; your card details do not pass through this website.
  • Chat. The "Ask us" chat finds its answers from this website's own pages, inside your browser. It remembers on your device that you have seen it. If you choose to send us a message through the chat, it is handled the same way as the enquiry form.

Other services this site uses

The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them. Links to Google Maps, Inland Revenue, Facebook, LinkedIn and our articles open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a privacy matter, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
