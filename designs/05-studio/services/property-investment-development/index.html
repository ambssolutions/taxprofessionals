Property has become harder

Property development in New Zealand has become increasingly difficult.

Successive governments have kept changing the laws on the taxation of property transactions, and have restricted the ability of businesses to move capital into and out of the country. Whether you are new to property investing or have built a large portfolio, an experienced property accountant can help you get the most from your returns and reach your rental or development property goals.

The team at Tax Professionals are experts in advising on property structures and managing property investors' affairs. Our Director, Ranjana Gupta, has published commentary, articles and chapters on New Zealand's land tax regime in accounting and tax textbooks and journals. Our Partner, Varun Gupta, is an avid property investor and developer with first-hand experience of the pitfalls and challenges in the current environment.

Family Trust Management & Advisory

Kiwis are increasingly using trusts to protect their assets. There are currently about 500,000 trusts in New Zealand.

Trusts are useful asset protection structures. A trust splits the ownership burdens and rights to a property between the trustees and the beneficiaries, which adds a layer of separation between a person and the asset. Trusts have been an important tool in asset planning for centuries, and as business and relationships have grown more complex they have become even more important.

The law of trusts is complex and changing, and recent legislation has considerably altered the disclosure requirements of some trusts. Our team keeps up to date with trust law, and we have staff trained in the management of trusts and estates. We can help you protect your most important assets with a well-structured, well-maintained trust.
