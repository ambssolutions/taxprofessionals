Business Advisory & Planning

Create a profitable, thriving and valuable business. Business planning starts with a first meeting free of charge.

Step outside your business

Our business advisors help you put a plan in place that lets you look at the bigger picture.

Businesses without a plan are likely to fail, especially those without a financial plan. When we work with business owners, we make sure they understand and put in place solid goal-setting. With those goals visible and agreed, we work on structuring the business so the goals will be achieved.

With regular progress monitoring, cashflow forecasts and careful budgeting, the business can stay on track with its plan, or change course for the better when it needs to. We measure our success by the success of the business owners we advise, which gives us every reason to take extra care of your progress.

  • Business plans and strategic growth
  • Performance and accountability
  • Profit improvement and cashflow
  • Systems and processes

Three ways we work with you

Start with a plan, keep it on track with a forecast, and have someone in your corner each month.

First meeting free of charge

Business Planning

A one-on-one workshop that shows you how to build a sustainable, practical plan for your business to guide the next three months, twelve months and three years. It covers business and personal goals, so the business serves the owner, not the other way around.

Cashflow Forecasting

Cashflow planning is best practice in any business and critical to survival and growth. Setting cashflow targets and regularly checking your actual cashflow against the forecast lets you predict large cash outflows and respond to changes in your business.

Monthly Coaching

Owning your own business is hard work. Monthly coaching gives you accountability and support in your planning and decision making, so you keep making progress. We help you work through roadblocks and challenges, combining our expertise with yours.

How you benefit from our coaching

  • You'll know where you are heading and what the goals are
  • You'll have a solid plan and an implementation strategy
  • Your finances will be visible to you at all times
  • You'll manage your time and get more done in less time
  • You'll build sharp leadership skills to manage your team
  • You'll automate your business so you can leave it for days

Your first meeting

It is vitally important to work on your business, not just in it. This meeting is a no-charge, no-obligation chance to talk about where the business is going.

What we can discuss

  • Your goals for the business and how you plan to achieve them
  • How your business goals line up with your personal goals
  • The challenges the business faces, such as growth, profitability, cash flow, asset protection, or your planned exit or sale
  • How we can work together to reach your goals and resolve those challenges

If needed, we also prepare a proposal for you, with upfront prices for any further support we identified in the meeting.

Points of discussion

  • Asset protection
  • Budgeting and cash flow forecasting
  • Business or share valuations
  • Business structuring
  • Buying a business
  • Financing
  • Management consultancy
  • Risk management
