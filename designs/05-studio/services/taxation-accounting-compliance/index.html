IRD disputes and tax debt

Whatever the tax position, talk to us. Getting on top of a tax problem early can help you avoid liquidation or bankruptcy because of tax debt.

Tax debt stays stressful until it is resolved with Inland Revenue. It is not only a civil liability: in some cases it can lead to criminal liability too, and Inland Revenue does prosecute tax evasion from time to time. That is why it is important to deal with tax problems quickly.

How we can help

  • Tax compliance reviews, IRD audits and liaison
  • Negotiating resolution of arrears in tax, such as GST, PAYE and income tax
  • Dealing with and responding to letters of demand from Inland Revenue

When you deal with Inland Revenue

  • Be honest
  • Keep communicating, meaningfully
  • Be transparent
  • Align your situation with the tax regime, for the best outcome available
  • Use an expert to negotiate with Inland Revenue. It improves the chances of a better outcome, and cheap can be expensive

Tax pooling through TMNZ

Pay provisional tax when it suits you.

Tax pooling is an IRD-approved service offered by our partner, Tax Management NZ (TMNZ). It eases some of the provisional tax system's challenges by giving taxpayers another way to pay their income tax.

One of the big problems with provisional tax is that Inland Revenue expects payments on the dates it sets, no matter when a business earns its income or how its cashflow looks, and it charges late payment penalties when a payment is missed. Whether tax pooling suits your situation is something we will go through with you.

Standard due dates are on the homepage, and you can work out tax, GST and provisional tax with our calculators.
