Employment & HR Advisory

Your employment law and HR partner. The most valuable asset in your business is your staff.

Book a chator call 09 625 0035

Support that fits your business

Every business is unique, so we tailor our support and approach to your situation.

In recognition of how much your staff matter, and to help you make the most of your business, we provide employment law and HR advisory services. We offer the full range of employment relations and conflict resolution services, from recruitment and grievances to redundancy and everything in between.

Businesses on our compliance package can add Employment & HR Advisory to it. See the Taxation & Accounting Compliance packages.

What we help with

Five areas, from the paperwork you need on day one to a visit from a Labour Inspector.

01

Employment relations and policies

Advice and help with employment agreements, employment legislation, and employee policies and procedures.

02

Employer accreditation

Accreditation has been mandatory since 2021. Avoid lengthy delays in your application: talk to us to get started on the accreditation process.

03

Restructure and redundancy

Looking to create change or restructure your business? We guide you through the correct process under employment law and help with:

  • Document creation
  • Consultation with staff
  • Redeployment options
  • Possible redundancies
  • Selection process criteria
04

Personal grievances and disputes

If your business receives a personal grievance, we can help with advice, options and managing your response through the process.

05

Audit and investigations

Practical, straightforward suggestions for managing your records and keeping the information a Labour Inspector will ask for if you are audited.

If a Labour Inspector visits, we can help you through the process to prevent Enforceable Undertakings being issued against your organisation.

Payroll alongside HR

Full PAYE and payroll services can be added to our business compliance package, or used on their own.
