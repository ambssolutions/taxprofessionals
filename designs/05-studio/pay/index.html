Visa and Mastercard. For debit cards, check that the issuer of the card allows online payments. Card payments may include a credit card charge; bank transfer has none.

How paying online works

Four steps, start to finish. Your card details go to Ezidebit, a Global Payments company, and never pass through our website.

Step 1 of 4

Find your invoice number and amount

Both are on your invoice. Keep it open, or copy the invoice number into the box at the top of this page so it is ready to paste.

Step 2 of 4

Select Pay online by card

Ezidebit's secure payment page opens in a new tab. Check that the address starts with https and shows a padlock.

Step 3 of 4

Enter your details on Ezidebit's page

First your details: individual or business, your name (and company name for a business), email and mobile. Then the payment: paste your invoice number into Bill/Invoice Reference, enter the amount in NZD, and your card number, expiry, name on card and CCV. Select Proceed.

Step 4 of 4

Confirm, and you are done

Ezidebit shows your details once more to confirm. Confirm them and the payment is complete. We match it to your invoice using the reference.

Questions about paying

Is it safe to pay online?

Yes. Your card details go to Ezidebit's secure payment page, not to our website. Ezidebit is a Global Payments company.

Is there a charge for paying by card?

Card payments may include a credit card charge. To avoid it, pay by bank transfer using your invoice number as the reference; the account details are on your invoice.

Can I pay by debit card?

Yes, with a Visa or Mastercard debit card. Check that the issuer of the card allows online payments.

What reference should I use?

Your invoice number. It is on your invoice, and it lets us match your payment to the right invoice. Use the same reference for a bank transfer.

Can someone take my payment over the phone?

Yes. Between 9:30am and 5:30pm, call 09 625 0025 or 09 625 0035 and press 0 for reception. Have your card handy.
