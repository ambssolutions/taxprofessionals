Every month

  • 28th: GST, the month after the period ends (except November and March periods)
  • 20th: PAYE paid, for small employers
  • Payday filing: employment information within 2 working days of payday

General dates from Inland Revenue. Tax payments must be made on or before the due date. Where the due date falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the payment is due on the next working day. GST is an exception and will always be payable on the last working day of the taxable period. Depending on your individual circumstances the due dates that apply to you may be different. Worth asking first

What happens when I first get in touch?

We will ask a few questions about your situation, such as your income, any property, a business or a trust, and arrange a time to talk it through. The first consultation is free, on all matters.

Can I meet you at the office?

You are welcome to, at 620 Manukau Road in Epsom, Monday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm or Saturday 11:30am to 3:00pm. We are centrally located with abundant free on-site parking. Please call first so we can set a time.

Can you look after a family trust or a rental property?

Yes. Family trust management and advisory is one of our services, with staff trained in trust and estate management, and property structuring is a specialty: personal ownership, a look-through company or a trust.

I owe Inland Revenue money. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and IRD audits, negotiate arrears for GST, PAYE and income tax, and respond to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

What is tax pooling, and is it for me?

We are a TMNZ Premium Partner. Tax pooling through TMNZ lets you pay provisional tax when it suits you. Whether it fits your situation is something we can go through with you.
