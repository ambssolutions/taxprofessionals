The tax year, quarter by quarter

The New Zealand tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March. These are Inland Revenue's standard dates for a 31 March balance date.

7 April
Terminal taxFor clients of a tax agent with an extension of time
7 May
Provisional tax, third instalmentStandard method
7 May
GST return for periods ending March

7 July
Income tax returns (IR3 and others)When you do not have a tax agent
28 August
Provisional tax, first instalmentStandard method

Each month
No annual standard dates fall in this quarterMonthly GST and PAYE dates still apply

15 January
Provisional tax, second instalmentStandard method
15 January
GST return for periods ending November
7 February
Terminal taxWithout an extension of time
31 March
Income tax returns for clients of a tax agentFor the previous tax year

Every month

  • 28th: GST, the month after the period ends (except November and March periods)
  • 20th: PAYE paid, for small employers
  • Payday filing: employment information within 2 working days of payday

General dates from Inland Revenue. Due on a weekend or public holiday? Pay on the next working day. GST is the exception: it is due on the last working day of the period. Depending on your individual circumstances the due dates that apply to you may be different; we will confirm yours. Calculators and forms are on our client resources page.

Worth asking first

A few things people often want to know first.

What happens when I first get in touch?

We will ask a few questions about your situation, such as your income, any property, a business or a trust, and arrange a time to talk it through. The first consultation is free, on all matters.

Can I meet you at the office?

You are welcome to, at 620 Manukau Road in Epsom, Monday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm or Saturday 11:30am to 3:00pm, with free on-site parking. Please call first so we can set a time.

Can you look after a family trust or a rental property?

Yes. Family trust management and advisory is one of our services, with staff trained in trust and estate management, and property structuring is a specialty: personal ownership, a look-through company or a trust. Tell us what you have now and we will talk about what would help.

I owe Inland Revenue money. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and IRD audits, negotiate arrears for GST, PAYE and income tax, and respond to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

What is tax pooling, and is it for me?

We are a TMNZ Premium Partner. Tax pooling through TMNZ is one way to manage provisional tax payments. Whether it suits you depends on your situation, so ask us and we will go through it with you.
