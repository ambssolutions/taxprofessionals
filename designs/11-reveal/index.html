Good to know

Short answers to common questions.

Is the first meeting really free?

Yes. The first consultation is free on all matters, so you can talk through your income, property, business or trust with us first.

Can I come to the office?

Yes, at 620 Manukau Road in Epsom, Monday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm or Saturday 11:30am to 3:00pm. We are centrally located with abundant free on-site parking.

I owe Inland Revenue money. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and IRD audits, negotiate arrears for GST, PAYE and income tax, and respond to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

How do your fees work?

We offer fixed fee services, so there are no shock bills, with support and service packages tailored to your needs.

What is tax pooling?

Tax pooling through Tax Management NZ (TMNZ) lets you pay provisional tax when it suits you. We can go through whether it suits your situation.
