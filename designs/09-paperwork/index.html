Things people ask us first

Short answers. Every situation is a little different, so we will confirm the details with you.

Q1When is my income tax return due?

For a 31 March balance date the general Inland Revenue date is 7 July. Clients of a tax agent usually have until 31 March the following year. Your own date may differ, so we will confirm it with you.

Q2Can you do my GST and PAYE as well as my income tax?

Yes. GST returns and PAYE returns are part of our tax compliance work, and we also offer bookkeeping and payroll services.

Q3I own a rental property. Is that something you handle?

Property is one of our services. We give advice on property structures and look after property investors' affairs. Tell us what you own and what you are planning, and we can talk it through.
