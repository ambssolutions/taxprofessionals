Dates worth knowing

Standard Inland Revenue dates for a 31 March balance date. The tax year runs 1 April to 31 March.

Standard Inland Revenue dates
DateWhat is dueWho it applies to
7 AprTerminal tax, with an extension of timeClients of a tax agent
7 MayGST for periods ending March. Provisional tax, third instalmentGST-registered; provisional taxpayers
7 JulIncome tax returns (IR3 and others)People who file without a tax agent
28 AugProvisional tax, first instalmentProvisional taxpayers, standard method
15 JanGST for periods ending November. Provisional tax, second instalmentGST-registered; provisional taxpayers
7 FebTerminal taxAnyone with tax left to pay for the year
31 MarIncome tax returns, extendedClients of a tax agent
28thGST, the month after each period ends (except the two above)GST-registered
20thPAYE paid for the previous month. Payday filing within 2 working days of each paydaySmall employers

General dates from Inland Revenue. Due on a weekend or public holiday? Pay the next working day (GST: the last working day of the period). We’ll confirm your own dates. client resources page.

Before your first visit

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation is free on all matters. For Business Planning, the first meeting is a one-on-one workshop, free of charge.

What should I bring?

Your IRD number, any recent letters from Inland Revenue, last year's return if you have it, and your records for the year: income, expenses and bank statements. For a rental, bring the rent and costs. If you are not sure, call and we will tell you what we need.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Usually, yes. Tell us who looks after you now, and we will explain how we become your tax agent with Inland Revenue and which records we need.

I owe Inland Revenue. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews, IRD audits and liaison, negotiating arrears of GST, PAYE and income tax, and responding to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

Which accounting software do you work with?

Xero and MYOB. Business compliance packages include free training and ongoing support on your accounting software.
