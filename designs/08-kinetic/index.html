DateWhat is dueNotes

28MTHGSTThe 28th of the month after your GST period ends
20MTHPAYE paidBy the 20th of the following month, for small employers. Payday filing within 2 working days of payday.

The NZ tax year runs 1 April to 31 March

General dates from Inland Revenue. Tax payments must be made on or before the due date. Where the due date falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the payment is due on the next working day. GST is an exception and will always be payable on the last working day of the taxable period. Depending on your individual circumstances the due dates that apply to you may be different; we will confirm them. Client resources

Fair questions

What do I need to bring for my tax return?

It depends on your income and what you own. Call or email us first and we will tell you what to gather for your situation.

Does using a tax agent change my due dates?

Inland Revenue gives clients of a tax agent later dates for some things, such as income tax returns, which move from 7 July to 31 March the following year. Whether that applies to you depends on your situation, so we will confirm your own dates.

Can you help with rental property and trusts?

Yes. We give advice on property structures, manage property investors' affairs and provide trust administration. Tell us what you own and we can talk it through.

Do you offer tax pooling?

Yes, through Tax Management NZ (TMNZ), so you can pay provisional tax when it suits you. We are a TMNZ Premium Accounting Partner. Ask us whether it suits your situation.

I owe Inland Revenue money. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and audits, negotiate GST, PAYE and income tax arrears, and respond to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.
