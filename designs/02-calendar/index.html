Questions about dates and getting started

Which dates apply to me?

It depends on how you earn, whether you are GST-registered, whether you employ staff and your balance date. The dates on this page are the standard ones. Once we know your situation, we will confirm your own dates.

What should I bring when I first come in?

Your IRD number, any recent letters from Inland Revenue, last year's return if you have it, and your records for the year: income, expenses and bank statements. For a rental, bring the rent and costs. If you are not sure, call us and we will tell you what we need.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Usually, yes. Tell us who looks after you now, and we will explain how we would become your tax agent with Inland Revenue and which records we would need. We will confirm what is involved once we have looked at your situation.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes, on all matters. For Business Planning the first meeting is a one-on-one workshop, free of charge.

I owe Inland Revenue. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews, IRD audits and liaison, negotiating arrears of GST, PAYE and income tax, and responding to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

Does having a tax agent change my dates?

It can. Inland Revenue generally gives clients of a tax agent until 31 March of the following year to file their income tax return, instead of 7 July, and terminal tax can move from 7 February to 7 April with an extension of time. We will confirm what applies to you.
