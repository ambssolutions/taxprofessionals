Tax dates through the year

The New Zealand tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March. These are the standard dates for a 31 March balance date.

  1. 1April

    The tax year starts.

  2. 7July

    Income tax returns due if you do not use a tax agent.

  3. 28August

    First provisional tax instalment.

  4. 15January

    Second provisional tax instalment. GST for periods ending November.

  5. 7February

    Terminal tax due.

  6. 31March

    The tax year ends. Returns due for tax agent clients with an extension.

  7. 7April

    Terminal tax for tax agent clients with an extension of time.

    Following year
  8. 7May

    Third provisional tax instalment. GST for periods ending March.

    Following year

GST

Due on the 28th of the month after your GST period ends, except periods ending November (15 January) and March (7 May).

PAYE

Payday filing within 2 working days of payday. Small employers pay PAYE by the 20th of the following month.

General dates from Inland Revenue. Due on a weekend or public holiday? Pay on the next working day. GST is the exception: it is due on the last working day of the period. Your own dates may differ; we will confirm them. Client resources

Before you come in

A few things people often ask us on the phone.

Can I drop in to the office?

You are welcome at 620 Manukau Road, Epsom, Monday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm and Saturday 11:30am to 3:00pm, with free on-site parking. Please call 09 625 0035 first so we can make sure the right person is free to see you.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

It depends on what you need help with, so call or email and we will tell you. Your IRD number and any recent letters from Inland Revenue are a good start. The first consultation is free on all matters.

My return is due on 7 July. Is it too late to get help?

Clients of a tax agent usually have until 31 March the following year to file. Whether that applies to you depends on your situation, so talk to us as early as you can and we will confirm your dates.

I have a letter from Inland Revenue about tax debt. Can you help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and audits, negotiating arrears for GST, PAYE and income tax, and responding to letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

How are your fees set?

We offer fixed fee services and packages tailored to what you need, so there are no shock bills. Fees for rental property work vary with the size of your portfolio. Ask us and we will explain before any work starts.
