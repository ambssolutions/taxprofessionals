Stops on the tax year

The New Zealand tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March. These are the standard stops for a 31 March balance date.

  1. 1 AprilThe tax year starts
  2. 7 JulyIncome tax returns due if you do not use a tax agent
  3. 28 AugustFirst provisional tax instalment
  4. 15 JanuarySecond provisional tax instalment. GST for periods ending November
  5. 7 FebruaryTerminal tax due
  6. 31 MarchThe tax year ends. Returns due for tax agent clients with an extension
  7. 7 AprilNext year Terminal tax for tax agent clients with an extension of time
  8. 7 MayNext year Third provisional tax instalment. GST for periods ending March

GST, most months

Due on the 28th of the month after your GST period ends, except periods ending November (15 January) and March (7 May).

PAYE, every payday

Payday filing within 2 working days of payday. Small employers pay PAYE by the 20th of the following month.

General dates from Inland Revenue. Your own dates may differ; we will confirm them.

Before you come in

A few things people often ask us on the phone.

Can I drop in to the office?

You are welcome at 620 Manukau Road, Epsom. Please call 09 625 0035 first so we can make sure the right person is free to see you.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

It depends on what you need help with, so call or email and we will tell you. Your IRD number and any recent letters from Inland Revenue are a good start.

My return is due on 7 July. Is it too late to get help?

Clients of a tax agent usually have until 31 March the following year to file. Whether that applies to you depends on your situation, so talk to us as early as you can and we will confirm your dates.

Can you help with my rental property?

Yes. We prepare income tax returns that include rental income, and we give advice on property structures. Every situation is different, so we will talk it through with you before suggesting anything.

Do you look after payroll for small employers?

Yes. We offer payroll services and PAYE returns, and we are a Crystal Payroll recommended partner. Tell us how many people you pay and how often, and we can talk about what would suit you.
