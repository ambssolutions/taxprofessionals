Payment dates in a standard tax year

For a 31 March balance date. The tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Payment and filing dates in a standard tax year
DateWhat falls dueFor tax-agent clients
7 JulyIncome tax returns (IR3 and others)Extended to 31 March the following year
28 AugustProvisional tax, 1st instalment (standard method)Same date
15 JanuaryProvisional tax, 2nd instalmentGST for periods ending NovemberSame date
7 FebruaryTerminal tax7 April, with an extension of time
7 MayProvisional tax, 3rd instalmentGST for periods ending MarchSame date
Every monthPAYE paid by the 20th (small employers). GST on the 28th of the month after the period ends.Same dates

General dates from Inland Revenue. Your own dates may differ; we will confirm them. Tax payments must be made on or before the due date. Where the due date falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the payment is due on the next working day. GST is an exception and is always payable on the last working day of the taxable period.
