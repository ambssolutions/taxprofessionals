07 / Questions

Before you engage us

Straight answers. The details depend on your situation, and we will go through them with you.

Q1Do you work with companies and trusts as well as individuals?

Yes. We prepare income tax returns for individuals, companies, trusts and partnerships, and we offer trust formation and management and company formation.

Q2We are taking on our first employee. Can you help?

We offer payroll and PAYE, employment agreements and policies, and employer accreditation. Payday filing is due within 2 working days of each payday, so it is worth talking to us before the first pay run.

Q3Can you help with tax pooling?

We are a TMNZ Premium Partner. Ask us whether tax pooling suits your provisional tax.

Q4Are the dates on this page my dates?

They are the general Inland Revenue dates for a 31 March balance date. Your own dates may differ, for example with a different balance date or an extension as a tax-agent client. We will confirm them with you.

Q5We have fallen behind with Inland Revenue. What now?

Whatever the tax position, talk to us. We help with compliance reviews, IRD audits, negotiating arrears of GST, PAYE and income tax, and responding to letters of demand.
