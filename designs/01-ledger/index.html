Inland Revenue dates, in order

fo. 12

The New Zealand tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March. These are the standard dates for a 31 March balance date, entered in the order they fall.

Standard Inland Revenue dates for a 31 March balance date
DateEntryApplies to
7 AprTerminal tax, with an extension of timeClients of a tax agent
7 MayGST for periods ending March. Provisional tax, third instalmentGST-registered; provisional taxpayers
7 JulIncome tax returns (IR3 and others)People who file without a tax agent
28 AugProvisional tax, first instalmentProvisional taxpayers, standard method
15 JanGST for periods ending November. Provisional tax, second instalmentGST-registered; provisional taxpayers
7 FebTerminal taxAnyone with tax left to pay for the year
31 MarIncome tax returns, extendedClients of a tax agent
28thGST, the month after each period ends (except the two above)GST-registered
20thPAYE paid for the previous month. Payday filing within 2 working days of each paydaySmall employers

04

Notes to the accounts

fo. 13

Questions people often ask before they become clients.

n. 1What should I bring to the first meeting?

Your IRD number, last year's return if you have it, and any letters from Inland Revenue. Add the records for the year: income, expenses and bank statements, and for a rental, the rent and costs. If you are unsure, call us and we will tell you what we need for your situation.

n. 2Can you take over from my previous accountant?

Usually, yes. Tell us who looked after you before. We can then talk you through becoming your tax agent with Inland Revenue and the records we would need. We will confirm what is involved once we have looked at your situation.

n. 3Do you only work with businesses?

No. We prepare returns for individuals and families as well as companies, trusts and partnerships, and we look after property investors' affairs.

n. 4What changes when you are my tax agent?

Inland Revenue generally gives clients of a tax agent more time: income tax returns are due by 31 March of the following year instead of 7 July. Your own dates can differ, and we will confirm them with you.

n. 5Can you help with staff and payroll?

Yes. We offer payroll services and PAYE returns, and advice on employment law and HR. We are a Crystal Payroll recommended partner.
