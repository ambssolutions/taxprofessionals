    Dates your business works to

    The standard Inland Revenue dates for a 31 March balance date, in one place.

    28thmonth after

    GST

    Due on the 28th of the month after your GST period ends. Periods ending in November are due 15 January. Periods ending in March are due 7 May.

    20thfollowing month

    PAYE

    Payday filing within 2 working days of each payday. Small employers pay PAYE by the 20th of the following month.

    28August
    15January
    7May

    Provisional tax

    Standard method, paid in three instalments.

    7July

    Income tax returns

    Due 7 July. Clients of a tax agent have until 31 March the following year.

    7February

    Terminal tax

    Due 7 February, or 7 April for tax-agent clients with an extension of time.

    The NZ tax year runs 1 April to 31 March

    General dates from Inland Revenue. Tax payments must be made on or before the due date. Where the due date falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the payment is due on the next working day. GST is an exception and will always be payable on the last working day of the taxable period. Depending on your individual circumstances the due dates that apply to you may be different; we will confirm them. Client resources

    Before you get in touch

    Do you work with sole traders as well as companies?

    Yes. We prepare income tax returns for individuals, companies, trusts and partnerships, and GST and PAYE returns for businesses of different shapes. Tell us how your business is set up and we will talk it through.

    Can you run our payroll?

    Yes, as an add-on to the business package or on its own. We are a Crystal Payroll recommended partner. Let us know how many people you pay and how often, and we can discuss what would suit you.

    Can I move to you from another accountant?

    Get in touch and tell us where things are up to. We will explain what a change involves for your situation before anything happens.

    What is tax pooling, and do you offer it?

    Tax pooling through Tax Management NZ (TMNZ) lets you pay provisional tax when it suits you. We are a TMNZ Premium Accounting Partner. Whether it suits you depends on your provisional tax, so ask us and we will look at your situation.

    Is the first consultation really free?

    Yes. The first consultation is free on all matters. Call 09 625 0035 or send the form below and we will set a time.

    Can I come into the office?

    We are at 620 Manukau Road, Epsom, centrally located with abundant free on-site parking. Reception is open Monday to Friday, 9:30am to 5:30pm, and Saturday, 11:30am to 3:00pm. Please call before you visit so the right person is available.

    How do I pay an invoice?
