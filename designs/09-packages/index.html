Due dates in a standard tax year

For a 31 March balance date. The tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

  • 7 July

    Income tax returns
    31 March the following year for tax-agent clients

  • 28 August

    Provisional tax, 1st instalment
    Standard method

  • 15 January

    Provisional tax, 2nd instalment
    Also GST for periods ending November

  • 7 February

    Terminal tax
    7 April for tax-agent clients with an extension of time

  • 7 May

    Provisional tax, 3rd instalment
    Also GST for periods ending March

  • Monthly

    GST on the 28th, PAYE by the 20th
    GST the month after the period ends; PAYE for small employers. Payday filing within 2 working days

General dates from Inland Revenue. Your own dates may differ; we will confirm them. Tax payments must be made on or before the due date. Where the due date falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the payment is due on the next working day. GST is an exception and is always payable on the last working day of the taxable period. Common questions

Short answers. We confirm the details for your situation at the first consultation, which is free.

What does "Enquire for monthly fee" mean?

The fee depends on your situation. For rental property it varies with the size of the portfolio. We agree a fixed fee with you before we start, so there are no shock bills later.

Can I add payroll or HR to the business package?

Yes. Full PAYE and payroll, and Employment & HR Advisory, are add-ons to the business package. Payroll is through our Crystal Payroll partnership.

I am behind with Inland Revenue. Can you still help?

Yes. We help with compliance reviews and audits, negotiate arrears of GST, PAYE and income tax, and answer letters of demand. Whatever the tax position, talk to us.

When is my income tax return due?

For a 31 March balance date the general Inland Revenue date is 7 July. Clients of a tax agent usually have until 31 March the following year. We confirm your own date with you.
