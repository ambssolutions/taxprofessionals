06

Questions

Short answers about working with us. For anything specific to you, call or send an enquiry.

6.1I have never used an accountant for my tax. Where do I start?

Call or send an enquiry with a line about your situation: salary, rental property, a business or a trust. We will talk it through with you and tell you what we would need to see.

6.2Does using a tax agent change my filing date?

Inland Revenue's standard date for income tax returns is 7 July. For clients of a tax agent it is generally extended to 31 March the following year.

Your own dates depend on your situation, so we will confirm them with you.

6.3Can you help with a rental property or a property trust?

Yes. Property is one of our services: advice on property structures and managing property investors' affairs. We also handle trust administration.

6.4I run a small business with staff. What can you take on?

GST and PAYE returns, bookkeeping, payroll services and employment law and HR advisory. We are a Crystal Payroll recommended partner. Tell us what you do now and we will suggest where we could help.
